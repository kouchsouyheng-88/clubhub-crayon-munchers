## Working agreements
1. Pull requests are reviewed by one other member within 24h and will be merged by the author after approval.
2. Nobody shall push directly to main. All works will be done on a seperate branch and merged through pull requests.
3. Everyone replies to messages within 12h on weekdays (or 24h on weekends).
4. Work is finished at least 24 hours before the deadline so that there is time for reviews.
5. The project builds with zero warnings before anyone opens a pull request.

## Decision rules
- Product questions: Product owner decides by asking for everyone's opinions in chat.
- Technical questions: Reach a concensus within 3-5 hours. If not, majority vote wins.
- Deadlock (no agreement after 24 hours): Consult with lecturer in the next session or through chat.
- Where decisions are recorded: lab01/decisions.md
