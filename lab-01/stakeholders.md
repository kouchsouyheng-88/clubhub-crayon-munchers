# Stakeholders · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 2026-10-16)
| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Needs from Club Hub | How we reach them |
|-------------|----------|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| ★ Students | High | Medium | Find events, register and cancel quickly, get a reminder | Short survey in class, 3 interviews |
| ★ Club leaders | High | High | Register the club, publish events, see who will come | Interview 2 leaders, demo of the flow |
| ★ Administrators | Medium | High | Approve clubs, see a monthly activity report across clubs | Meeting with the administrator or via the TA |
| Organisers | High | Medium | Check students in at the door, see attendance live | Observe one event, interview |
| Client (instructor / TA) | High | High | A product that matches the case-study brief and course goals | Weekly lab session, Product Owner talks to the client |
| Institute IT staff | Low | Medium | Data handled safely, nothing that burdens their systems | Email questions through the TA |
| Our team (developers) | High | High | Clear requirements, a build that works, fair workload | Chat, weekly stand-up, GitHub Issues |
| Student data owners / privacy (institute policy) | Low | Medium | Student ID, e-mail and phone are protected | Ask the TA which policy applies |
