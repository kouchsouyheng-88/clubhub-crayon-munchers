# Privacy-Notice
We are the Club Hub team at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC). The ITC Clubs Office is responsible for managing your personal information. We collect your student ID and name just to identify who you are, as well as to manage your account and registration. We take your email to ensure that you will get confirmations from our app or website and to inform you about some essential information that you should not miss. For your phone number, it is optional, but if you provide it, you will get SMS reminders from us. We also keep your club membership to manage your club activities and attendance records to check-in your participation. The ITC Clubs Office can access your information to manage the system and prepare monthly reports. Authorized club leaders and organizers can see the information needed to manage their clubs, events and attendance. Sponsors cannot access your personal information. We will keep your personal information as long as it is necessary for ITC Club Hub. We remove the data when it becomes unnecessary. Attendance information may be retained without any personal details of any individual student. Providing your phone number and allowing SMS alerts to be sent to you is entirely voluntary. Optional email alerts are not activated unless you choose to do so. Refusal of optional uses of your data is allowed at any time. If you have questions about your personal information or want to withdraw your consent, contact the ITC Clubs Office.
